Couples Retreat · June 19–26

Reconnect. Slow down. Remember what brought you together.

Set against the sun-drenched beauty of Puglia, The Italian Reset Couples Retreat is an intimate escape designed for couples who want to step away from the noise of everyday life and reconnect with themselves, with each other, and with the simple pleasures of being together.

Surrounded by Puglia's golden landscapes, ancient olive groves, turquoise coastline, and warm Italian hospitality, couples will have the space to breathe, reflect, laugh, explore, and simply enjoy one another without the distractions of daily responsibilities.

This retreat combines wellness, connection, meaningful experiences, and la dolce vita. Through guided reflection, mindful practices, shared experiences, delicious food, cultural exploration, and time to relax, couples are invited to slow down and create space for deeper communication and intimacy.

There is no pressure to fix, change, or become anything. This is about coming back to each other with curiosity, presence, playfulness, and appreciation.

From quiet mornings and sunset moments to shared meals, local experiences, and time by the sea, every element of the retreat is designed to help couples reconnect and create memories they will carry home with them.

The Italian Reset is more than a holiday. It is an opportunity to pause, reconnect, and rediscover the joy of being together.

Before you book

Quick answers.

The short version. Full FAQ & policies are linked below.

What's the difference between the two retreats?

The Women's Retreat (June 28–July 4) is a women-only group of 10, each in a private room. The Couples Retreat (June 19–26) is for 6 couples, each sharing a private double room. Same location, same team, different focus — individual reconnection versus reconnecting as a couple.

Do I have to know anyone going in?

On the Women's Retreat, no — most guests arrive solo, and the retreat is designed to bring people together from the first dinner. The Couples Retreat is booked as a pair, so you'll already know your plus-one.

What's the physical activity level?

Gentle. Morning movement and walking — nothing strenuous. You don't need to be an athlete or a yogi.

What's the cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable. 90+ days out: full credit toward a future retreat. 30–89 days: 50% refund. Under 30 days: non-refundable. Travel insurance strongly recommended.

Why so few spots?

Because real connection doesn't happen in a crowd. The Women's Retreat is capped at 10, the Couples Retreat at 6 couples — small enough that everyone knows everyone's name by Day 2.

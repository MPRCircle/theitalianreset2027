The week

Come to Monopoli to reset, release the layers of stress, and more joy.

Water is elemental for renewal and healing. That's why we've chosen this villa, as it's right by the sea. Mornings with meditation, movement, and journaling. Workshops on freeing your natural voice. This retreat is not meant to be jam-packed with activities.

Dinner is the main event. Everyone sits down together, and nobody's rushing to leave.

This retreat is made to give you more energy. More freedom. And more restful moments to recharge.

This is a space for BIPOC and diverse women who are ready to put their own joy first. You don't need to be sporty, spiritual or good at Italian. You just need to want a break.

Day by day

What the week looks like.

It's a loose plan, not a packed schedule. Here's the rough shape.

1Arrive

Land and settle in

We pick you up, you get settled in your room, and you meet everyone. The welcome dinner is easy. Good food, nothing to do but eat and talk.

2–3Settle

Find your rhythm

Mornings start with easy movement. Afternoons are for a cooking class, journaling circles and your first walk through the village. Dinners run long. Leave your phone in your room.

4–5Explore

Get out and about

A morning at the local market. A walk through a vineyard. One free afternoon to rest or roam. Then sunset together.

6Reflect

Take stock

A slower day to talk through the week. What do you want to keep? What are you leaving here?

7Depart

Say goodbye

One last breakfast together, then we get you to the airport. Expect long hugs and a very active group chat.

Questions

The things people ask first.

How much do I pay to reserve my spot?

Just a €250 deposit. It holds your place, and it's non-refundable. You pay the rest in full or in installments. The total is €6,300.

Where is the retreat?

At Villa de Bellis in Monopoli, a coastal town in Puglia, Italy. You can see the villa in the video above.

Do I need to know anyone?

No. Most women come on their own. By the first dinner, you'll have people to sit with.

How active is it?

Not very. Easy morning movement and some walking. You don't need to be an athlete or a yogi.

Is it spiritual or religious?

No. The reflection circles are down to earth and not tied to any belief system.

I've never traveled abroad. Is that okay?

Yes. We'll send you what you need before you go, and our team is with you the whole week. You won't be figuring it out alone.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel 90 or more days before the retreat and you get a full credit toward a future retreat. Cancel 30–89 days before and you get 50% back. Under 30 days, there's no refund. The deposit is non-refundable. We strongly suggest travel insurance.

Why only 10 women?

Because real connection doesn't happen in a crowd. With ten, everyone knows everyone's name by day two.
